# Design Space and Infra WhatsApp Follow-Up Templates

## New Enquiry Reply

`Hello, thank you for contacting Design Space and Infra. Please share your location, property type, and what interior work you are planning. We will guide you with the next step.`

## Site Visit Discussion

`Thank you for sharing the details. A site visit will help us understand measurements, existing conditions, scope, and practical execution needs before giving proper guidance.`

## Home Interior Lead

`For home interiors, we can help with space planning, modular kitchen, wardrobes, TV units, bedroom interiors, furniture, ceiling, lighting, and material selection. Please share the room photos or plan if available.`

## Commercial Interior Lead

`For commercial spaces, we usually start by understanding your business type, seating or usage requirement, brand look, timeline, and opening date. We can then guide you on the design and execution approach.`

## Budget Follow-Up

`Budget depends on measurements, materials, finishes, hardware, furniture scope, and site condition. If you share your rough budget comfort, we can suggest a practical direction before the site visit.`

## Gentle Follow-Up After Silence

`Hello, just checking if you would like to continue the discussion for your interior work. We can help with design, material guidance, execution planning, and site coordination.`

## Consultation Confirmation

`Your consultation request is received. Please share a convenient time for a call, and we will connect with you to understand the requirement clearly.`
